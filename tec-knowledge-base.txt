ABOUT TRENT EDUCATION CENTRE
=========================================================

Trent Education Centre (TEC) is an education provider that has offered courses to adults in Nottingham, Leicester and Birmingham since 2012.

TEC believes that the well-being and personal growth of students are essential to their success. Many of the people TEC supports believe they have missed their chance for education — they may have left school recently or many years ago, and lack academic skills or confidence. TEC provides a second chance.

TEC offers a holistic approach to education. Courses are delivered by qualified teachers with experience teaching adults.

TEC does NOT offer GCSEs. TEC offers the following courses:

--- ENGLISH LANGUAGE COURSES ---

1. Ascentis ESOL Certificate (English for Speakers of Other Languages)
   - Awarding Body: Ascentis
   - Location: Castle Boulevard campus, Nottingham
   - For: Adults aged 18+ living in the UK who want to improve their English
   - Suitable for: People from settled communities, refugees, asylum seekers, anyone who speaks another language at home
   - What you learn: English for work, daily life, UK services, travel, study and socialising
   - Levels available: Pre-Intermediate; Intermediate / Upper Intermediate
   - Schedule: 2 days per week, 4 hours per day (10:00–16:30)
   - Self-study available Monday and Tuesday 9:30–17:00 on campus
   - Three units: Speaking & Listening (15 weeks / 120 hours), Reading (6 weeks / 60 hours), Writing (9 weeks / 90 hours)
   - Total: 30 weeks, 270 hours
   - Exam: Teachers assess work; Ascentis checks the results (no formal exams from external exam board)
   - Follows the Adult ESOL Core Curriculum
   - Example intake: Starting February 2026 (with Easter break)
   - Fees: Contact TEC for current fee information
   - International students: Must pay an extra booking fee before travelling to UK (covers visa admin costs)
   - No extra fees for registration, exams, certificates, materials or activities
   - Maximum class size: Contact TEC for details

2. NCFE Functional Skills English (Level 1 & 2)
   - Awarding Body: NCFE
   - Duration: 6 Weeks / Onsite
   - Location: Castle Boulevard campus, Nottingham
   - For: Adults who want a nationally recognised English qualification
   - Levels: Level 1 and Level 2

3. IELTS Exam Preparation
   - Location: Castle Boulevard campus (purpose-designed English Language campus)
   - Intake: Every month
   - Covers all four IELTS sections: Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking
   - Teaches: exam techniques, time management, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation
   - Practice tests and mock exams with feedback from experienced instructors
   - Customised for Academic or General Training IELTS versions
   - IELTS test components:
     * Listening: 30 minutes + 10 minutes to transfer answers; 4 parts
     * Reading: 60 minutes; three passages
     * Writing: 60 minutes; two tasks
     * Speaking: 10–15 minutes face-to-face interview

ADMISSIONS
=========================================================

TEC's admissions process is fair, transparent, and gives all applicants equal opportunities. Applications are accepted regardless of background or circumstances. TEC actively promotes applications from under-represented communities and those who may face disadvantages or barriers to higher education.

Admissions Email: admissions@trenteducation.co.uk

Application Steps:
1. Complete the online Student Application Form (takes approximately 20 minutes) — include your details, course choice, and supporting documents
2. The admissions team reviews your application against entry requirements
3. If successful, you will receive an offer and can enrol and begin studying

Funding Options:
- Self-funded: You pay fees and maintenance costs yourself
- Sponsored: An employer or sponsor pays course fees for you
- Government grant or loan: You may be eligible for government funding
- Scholarships and Bursaries: Limited number available from TEC

Scholarships:
- For students who demonstrate outstanding achievements (academic, work, sports, arts, music)
- May cover the full costs of study
- Must be submitted at least one month before the term starts
- Decisions made by Senior Management Team (SMT) within two weeks

Bursaries:
- For students from low-income backgrounds
- Contributes towards the cost of studies
- Must be submitted at least one month before the term starts

TEC sets aside £100,000 per annum for scholarships and bursaries. Once spent for the academic year, no further applications accepted until the next academic year.

Eligibility and criteria: Contact admissions@trenteducation.co.uk
